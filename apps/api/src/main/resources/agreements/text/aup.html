ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (AUP)

Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") outlines the standards for using {{COMPANY_NAME}}, communication tools, data, and devices. It ensures responsible use, protects company data, upholds client confidentiality, and ensures compliance with applicable laws.

Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees, consultants, contractors, interns, and temporary staff;
  • All devices, communication tools, networks, systems, and data owned, managed, or provided by {{COMPANY_NAME}}.
  • All work-related communication and documentation conducted or stored using company systems, including remote or hybrid work environments.

Authorized Use Guidelines

Email and Communication Tools

  • Employees must use only company-provided email accounts for all communication with clients, vendors, consultants, and internal teams.
  • Gmail IDs or any email IDs', authorized by the company may be used only for approved consultant interactions.
  • Personal email accounts are strictly prohibited for any work-related communication, documentation, or approvals.

Use of Personal Devices and Phones

  • Employees shall not use personal mobile phones or messaging platforms (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS) to communicate with clients, consultants, or vendors unless explicitly authorized in writing by the Operations Manager.
  • WhatsApp Business communication must occur through WhatsApp Web on desktops, using company-approved numbers only.
  • Phones must be kept out of use during working hours unless required for official work and permitted by the reporting manager.

System and Network Usage

  • Devices provided by the company are to be used solely for professional purposes.
  • Accessing inappropriate websites, streaming platforms, pirated content, or gambling/betting sites is strictly prohibited.
  • Installation of unauthorized software or modification of company system configurations is not allowed.
  • Company Wi-Fi or VPN must not be used for illegal activities or personal financial transactions unrelated to employment.

Data Protection and Confidentiality

  • All confidential information, including intellectual property, client data, financials, and business records, must be safeguarded.
  • Unauthorized access, handling, or disclosure of company or client data is a serious violation and may invoke penalties under IT Act, 2000, including but limited to prosecution under applicable penal laws.
  • Forwarding official emails to personal accounts or third-party services is strictly forbidden.
  • Personnel engaged with sensitive and confidential information of the Company shall handle the same responsibly with utmost care and custody. Any leakage of data owing to negligence will be treated as breach of this policy.
  • Passwords must be secure, confidential, changed regularly, and not shared.
  • Any known or suspected data theft, leak, or unauthorized access must be reported immediately.

Ethics and Conduct

  • Employees must act with honesty, integrity, and respect for others and for company property.
  • Use of IT resources should reflect the company's commitment to professionalism and ethical standards.
  • Employees shall respect the Intellectual Property (trade secrets, business model) of the Company.
  • Reporting of any unauthorized incidents or breaches is obligatory.

Prohibited Activities

  • Using company information, systems, or communication tools for personal gain is strictly prohibited.
  • Soliciting consultants for money, favors, or services outside of official company channels is unethical and punishable.
  • Engaging in outside or personal business activities during official working hours is strictly prohibited.
  • Breach of trust and misuse of company time, resources, and infrastructure for non-company-related purposes is a direct violation of this policy.
  • Unauthorized access to systems, data, or accounts (hacking or privilege escalation).
  • Misrepresentation of identity or affiliation.
  • Installing or using unauthorized software or hardware.
  • Use of any unauthorized external devices.
  • Employees shall not indulge in replicating the business model, Intellectual Property, or trade secrets of the Company in any manner whatsoever.

Monitoring and Compliance

{{COMPANY_NAME}} reserves the right to monitor usage of its systems, email traffic, internet access, and data access, at all times, without any notice. The IT resources provided to the employees, shall be the sole propriety of the Company.

Monitoring will be performed to:

  • Ensure compliance with this policy
  • Investigate security breaches
  • Protect company and client interests.

Misuse of company resources, even outside working hours using company devices, is subject to disciplinary action.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Written warning
  • Suspension of access or assignment
  • Termination of employment or contract
  • Legal proceedings in accordance with applicable laws, viz., Information Technology Act, 2000, The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, & any other applicable laws.
  • Claim of liquidated damages towards the loss that may have been incurred or suffered by the Company as a result of any breach of this policy by the Employees.

Grievances and Exceptions

Employees may submit concerns or exception requests in writing to the HR or Operations department. Approved exceptions will be formally documented.

Acknowledgement

All users of {{COMPANY_NAME}} systems are required to acknowledge and consent to this policy as a condition of access.
