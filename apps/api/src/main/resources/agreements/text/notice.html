NOTICE PERIOD CONDUCT GUIDELINES

To ensure a smooth and professional transition, employees serving a notice period are required to comply with the following standards. These guidelines form part of the Employee Handbook and are binding until the last working day.

1. Policy Compliance:
Continue to follow all company policies, code of conduct, attendance rules, and confidentiality obligations until the last working day.

2. Professional Conduct:
Maintain professional conduct with colleagues, managers, consultants, clients, and vendors at all times, and refrain from any actions that may cause loss or damage to the company's reputation.

3. Work Responsibility:
The employee is required to Perform assigned duties diligently, complete pending tasks, and ensure proper knowledge transfer, documentation, and handover of responsibilities.

4. Knowledge Transfer & Handover:
A detailed handover of responsibilities, documents, credentials, and consultant's info must be provided to the concerned person. KT sessions must be attended as scheduled.

5. Attendance & Leave:
Unplanned leave should be avoided during the notice period. Any leave must be taken only with prior approval from the Reporting Manager/HR and may result in extension of the notice period.

6. Confidentiality & Data Security:
The employee shall not download, copy, delete, alter, or share any company data on personal devices or external platforms. All confidential and proprietary information must be handled and protected in compliance with the NDA and the Company's Data Security Policy.

7. Use of Company Assets:
Company assets (laptop, ID card, SIM, access cards, documents, etc.) must be used responsibly and returned in good condition before the last working day.

8. No Dual Employment:
The employee must not join another organization or engage in external employment/business during the notice period without written approval.

9. Client & Team Communication:
The employee should not independently inform vendor/clients about their exit unless authorized. All external communication must follow manager/HR guidance.

10. Exit Formalities:
Exit interview, IT clearance forms, and documentation must be completed. Full & Final settlement shall be initiated only upon completion of internal audit clearance, asset return, and proper handover of responsibilities.

11. Social Media Conduct:
Posting negative or sensitive information about the company, vendors, clients, consultants or colleagues on public platforms is prohibited and will result in prosecution by the Organization under Information Technology Act, 2000, DPDP Act, 2023, Indian Contract Act, 1872, including but not limited to criminal prosecution under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

12. Extension of Notice Period:
The company reserves the right to extend the notice period in cases of incomplete handover, unauthorized leave, misconduct, or policy violations.
