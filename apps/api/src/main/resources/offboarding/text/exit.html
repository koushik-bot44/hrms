Separation & Exit Formalities

Dear Valued Team Member,

Following a significant Data Security breach incident in 2025 that materially impacted client data integrity and business operations, the Company has instituted a mandatory internal audit protocol as part of the employee off-boarding process.

This audit serves as a risk-mitigation and compliance safeguard to identify potential data security vulnerabilities, unauthorized access, and other discrepancies.

Accordingly, a Caution Notice and the Company's Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) have been formally communicated to all employees to reinforce data protection and regulatory obligations.

Please be advised that the internal audit is now a standard and non-discretionary component of the off-boarding process for all employees separating from the organization.

This audit may include, but is not limited to:

  • Post-employment data verification
  • Client data compliance and integrity checks
  • Data exfiltration analysis
  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP) review

These measures are undertaken in the legitimate business interest of the Company to:

  • Safeguard confidential and proprietary information
  • Protect intellectual property rights
  • Prevent unauthorized disclosure or misuse of data
  • Ensure compliance with contractual, statutory, and regulatory obligations

Employee cooperation is part of the continuing duty of confidentiality and fiduciary responsibility owed to the Company.

To complete the audit, exit formalities may be extended by up to two (2) weeks. This is a procedural and compliance requirement and does not imply misconduct.

The Company reserves its rights under employment agreements, confidentiality obligations, and applicable laws to conduct such reviews in protection of its legitimate business interests.

We appreciate your cooperation and professionalism in supporting a secure, compliant transition.
